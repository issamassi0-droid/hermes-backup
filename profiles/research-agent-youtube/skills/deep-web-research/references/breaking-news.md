# Breaking News — Special Rules

## Application Principle
Events evolving within hours, initial sources incomplete.

## Precise Rules

1. **Freshness window**: Do not accept a source older than 6 hours for an ongoing event.
2. **Progressive nature**: Present the news as "developments" with timestamps.
3. **Cross-verification**: At least 3 independent sources for numbers/casualties.
4. **Official sources first**: Civil defense/health/foreign ministry.
5. **No silent updates**: If a number changes, mention the previous number.
